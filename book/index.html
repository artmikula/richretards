The Rule of 72

How fast your money doubles, in your head

Divide 72 by your yearly return and you get the rough number of years for your money to double. At 7% that is about every ten years. It is the fastest way to sanity check any promise anyone ever makes you.

🔒 The small repeatable move that quietly bends this curve is in Chapter 3.
Order of operations

Where your next dollar goes matters more than its size

There is a correct sequence for funding your future. Free matched money, then expensive debt, then a cash cushion, then long term growth. Get the order wrong and you leave real money on the table every single year.

🔒 The exact dollar by dollar sequence is laid out inside.
Real vs nominal

The number on the screen lies a little

A 7% return with 3% inflation is closer to 4% in what your money can actually buy. Most people quietly celebrate the wrong figure for decades. We teach you to think only in real buying power.

🔒 The simple way to protect against inflation without guessing is inside.
