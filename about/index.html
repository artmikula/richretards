💰 ABOUT RICH RETARDS 💰

🤔 Who The Hell Are We?

Rich Retards is a meme finance community born from the simple observation that people keep making the same stupid financial mistakes over and over — and nobody's documenting them in a way that's actually entertaining to read.

We're not financial advisors. We're not your accountant. We're not even particularly good with money. But we ARE really good at finding the most spectacularly bad financial decisions on the internet and turning them into content that might actually save you from doing the same dumb thing.

🎯 Our Mission

To make financial literacy so entertaining that people actually learn something. Traditional finance content is boring as hell — all spreadsheets and compound interest calculators. We think you'll learn more from watching someone lose their life savings on a meme coin than from reading a 47-page PDF about portfolio diversification.

Every scam we break down is a lesson. Every financial fail is a warning. Every "wisdom" piece has actual, usable advice hidden under layers of sarcasm and meme references.

📰 What We Cover

🚨 Scam Alerts — Crypto rug pulls, fake forex mentors, Ponzi schemes, phishing scams, and every flavor of financial fraud. We investigate, we expose, we meme.

📉 Financial Fails — Real stories of terrible money decisions. The guy who mortgaged his house for NFTs. The Redditor who YOLOd his 401K. The tenant who didn't read their lease. We document them all.

🧠 Actual Wisdom — Hidden fees in contracts, predatory clauses landlords hope you won't read, and practical advice on not getting financially destroyed. We even recommend tools like Fineprint for scanning contracts.

📧 The Newsletter

Every week, we send out a curated breakdown of the worst financial decisions, latest scams, and actually useful money lessons. It's free, it's funny, and your inbox will thank you. Sign up here.
